The scariest horror story in exactly two sentences. The program writes one story to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Every morning I wake my son for school by calling his name up the stairs, and every morning he answers from his room. This morning he answered from directly behind me.

## Why this story

- **Two sentences, genuinely frightening.** A morning routine every parent knows, broken in the last four words.
- **The second sentence turns the first.** "Every morning he answers from his room" was reassuring until today — if he answered from behind me, something else has been answering from his room all along.
- **Horror by implication, not explanation.** Nothing is named, nothing is described; the reader fills in the worst of it.
- **Precise and concrete.** One routine, one staircase, one voice — 167 characters, every word earning its place.
- **Original.** No mirror, no phone call from inside the house, no monster under the bed — just a voice in the wrong place.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
